About Me

Where public service meets technology and data.

My professional background began in public service, where I have spent seven years working in government roles involving case management, policy review, detailed documentation, and coordination across departments.

Through these experiences, I was able to develop strong skills in accuracy, organization, problem-solving, and analyzing information to support important decisions.

While working full-time, I earned my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, building a foundation in programming, databases, systems, and analytical thinking. I am now completing a master’s degree in Information Technology Management with a concentration in Data Science, where I am expanding my knowledge of data analysis, business intelligence, database design, and data-driven decision-making.

My background combines real-world government experience with technical education and a growing focus on using data and technology to understand problems, improve processes, and support better business decisions.
